Redpoint Field Services · Week 1, Guided Lab Part 1

Your pod has 35 minutes. Everything you need is in this pack. Nothing needs to be invented, and no figure here should be changed.

Suggested timing. 5 min read alone, no pod discussion · 12 min capability map · 8 min four lenses · 8 min the statement · 2 min pick who presents.

1. The company

Redpoint Field Services installs and maintains commercial heating, ventilation and cooling systems. Roughly 600 field engineers covering about 11,000 contracted sites - offices, hospitals, data centres and retail parks. Most revenue comes from annual maintenance contracts with service-level commitments attached.

Redpoint acquired a competitor in 2024 and still runs two field-service systems while the merger completes.

2. The request, exactly as it arrived

An email to the Head of Architecture, copied to the IT Director:

"Our engineers are completing too few jobs a day and the contract margins are going the wrong way. I want an AI scheduling optimiser this financial year. Uber can route thousands of drivers in real time - why can't we do the same for a van? Come back to me with what it would take."

- Chief Operating Officer

3. Operational data

All figures are from the last 12 months unless stated. Treat them as sourced.

One more data point, from the service desk log: of the customer complaints logged last year, the largest single category was "engineer attended but could not complete the work".

6. Constraints that already exist

Constraint Where it comes from What it rules out
Two field-service systems following the 2024 acquisition; consolidation runs into 2027 Post-acquisition integration Anything that assumes one authoritative job or asset record today
Engineer handhelds lose connectivity in plant rooms and basements, which is where much of the work happens Physical reality of the sites Any design that assumes a live connection at the moment of work
Union agreement on job allocation - material changes to how work is allocated to engineers require consultation before they take effect Collective agreement Anything that changes allocation rules unilaterally or at short notice

7. What your AI Pod produces

Write these four things. One person in the pod shares their screen and types; everyone keeps their own copy, because this is Parts 2 to 5 of your own Lab Kit, not just a pod exercise.

1. The capability map. Four to six capabilities from the table above. For each, mark where cost, friction or inefficiency concentrates and quote the number. Then say which are hot and which are genuinely fine - naming at least one healthy capability is required.

2. All four lenses on your hottest capability. Cost · Risk · Time-to-value · Adoption. Each tied to a named role from section 4, never "the business". Then run the swap test: cover the labels, shuffle the rows, do they still make sense? If yes, rewrite.

3. One Validated Problem Statement. A capability, a number from this pack, the consequence, a named role who carries it, and zero technology words. One sentence.

4. One line on context. Which named governance body or constraint from sections 5 and 6 most shapes what Redpoint can actually do here, and why.

8. Three things that will cost you marks

  1. Agreeing with the COO. He has given you a prescription. Check the data before you accept the diagnosis - and notice which capability his request actually points at.
  2. Listing activities instead of capabilities. "Send the reminder email" is an activity. "Collect a Payment" is a capability. The rubric caps this at 25 out of 100.
  3. A technology word in your statement. Strike every one out before you present. Two separate scored lines depend on technology being absent from this part of your work.

9. When you come back

One person per pod presents, in about two minutes:

  • Which capability you picked as hot, and the number behind it
  • Which one you found was genuinely fine
  • Your problem statement, read out loud, word for word

Then we open the model answer together. Nobody is marked today - this is a rehearsal for the work you finish and submit on Wednesday.
